# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

# Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Superstore sales data and uncover key business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional trends.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify top-performing products and customers, evaluate profitability, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

# Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- DAX

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales: $2.30M
- Total Profit: $286.40K
- Quantity Sold: 38K

---

# Dashboard Features

Sales Analysis

- Total Sales by Region
- Total Sales by State
- Year-wise Sales Trend Analysis

Profitability Analysis

- Profit and Discount by Sub-Category
- Sales and Profit Comparison by Product Category
- Identification of Loss-Making Categories

Customer Analysis

- Top 5 Customers by Sales
- Customer Revenue Contribution Analysis

---

# Key Insights

- Phones generated the highest sales revenue among all product categories.
- The West region recorded the highest overall sales performance.
- Sales and profit showed consistent growth from 2014 to 2017.
- Tables generated high sales but resulted in negative profit.
- High discount levels significantly impacted profitability in certain categories.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Reduce excessive discounting on low-profit categories.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories.
- Review pricing strategies for loss-making products such as Tables.
- Strengthen retention strategies for high-value customers.
